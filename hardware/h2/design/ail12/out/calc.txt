## 2 Proposed OVLO clamp "BZX84C1V5"
- BZX84 series (Diodes DS18001; onsemi BZX84C2V4LT1/D) starts at 2V4. No 1V5 type is listed: the MPN is treated as NON-EXISTENT/UNVERIFIED (Nexperia page not retrievable from here).

## 8 Proposal: AUXOFF -> BSS84 gate, source = P, drain = GND
- The PMOS conducts only while VSG = V(P) - V(AUXOFF) > |VGS(th)|. With AUXOFF at 0 V, P cannot fall below |VGS(th)|: min |VGS(th)| = 0.9 V at 250 uA, 25 C (DS) > 0.8 V. At the real sink current (~1.9 mA through R2) P settles higher still.
  |Vth| 0.9 V, AUXOFF low 0.0 V: P = 1.28 V (level-1 fit to RDS(on) max; BEHAVIOURAL)
  |Vth| 0.9 V, AUXOFF low 0.3 V: P = 1.56 V (level-1 fit to RDS(on) max; BEHAVIOURAL)
  |Vth| 2.0 V, AUXOFF low 0.0 V: P = 2.28 V (level-1 fit to RDS(on) max; BEHAVIOURAL)
  |Vth| 2.0 V, AUXOFF low 0.3 V: P = 2.56 V (level-1 fit to RDS(on) max; BEHAVIOURAL)
- RESULT: P <= 0.8 V is NOT guaranteed; it is guaranteed NOT to be reached at 25 C (P >= 0.9 V). Hot-temperature VGS(th) is not specified. The BSS84 can still pull P below 0.8 x VCB (CD trip ~3.9 V), so the CD path may shut the PA; P itself stays in an undefined logic region.

## 11 Timing (H2-D tail from P sink asserted to drain < 0.5 V: 16.50 us, budget.py)
- Guaranteed fallback: +5V_IL must decay to U3 UV at 5 % overdrive (4.37 V). With C <= 73 uF and load >= 4.5 mA (ASSUMED; P is still high so R2 does not load) this takes up to 11.5 ms, then tPD 30 us. From the short onset this is >> 50 us.
- Under the existing R4 definition (onset = monitored rail 5 % beyond its threshold) the +5V_IL row stays 46.5 us. During the decay +5V_IL is within ROC, so the permit logic remains valid; the PA stays enabled on a regulator that has already failed until then.
- If the onset is the short itself, the AUXOFF path must assert P within 50 - 16.5 = 33.5 us. AUXOFF de-assertion delay has NO specification -> not provable.
